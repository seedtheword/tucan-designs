How It Works

From rough lumber
to your room.

Every table follows the same careful path. No rushing, no shortcuts — just honest work and clear communication from first sketch to final delivery.

01

Design

We talk through your space, your style, and how you'll use the piece. You approve dimensions, wood, and finish before a single board is cut.

02

Build

Your table is cut, joined, and shaped by hand from solid hardwood. We share progress photos so you watch it come together.

03

Deliver

Finished, sealed, and protected. We coordinate careful local delivery or crated shipping so it arrives ready to gather around.

Brandon founded Tucan Designs with a simple belief: furniture should be built to outlive its makers. With years at the workbench, he designs and hand-builds every table, obsessing over joinery, grain, and the way a finish catches the light.
